FAQs

Questions Owners Ask Us First

Is WhatsApp marketing allowed?+

Yes, when you message customers who have ordered from you or opted in. We set it up the proper way and help you keep messages relevant.

How do I get my customers' numbers?+

Every direct order saves the number to your CRM. QR codes, parcel inserts and first order rewards bring app customers into it.

Can I send offers for festivals and slow days?+

Yes. Send festive deals, weekend specials and new menu updates whenever you want, to the whole list or a part of it.

Will customers get spammed?+

No. We keep the schedule sensible, and customers can opt out at any time.

Do I have to stop selling on Zomato and Swiggy?+

No. Keep them for new customers. RestroGrow moves your regulars to direct orders.

How does the 3X guarantee work?+

Over 90 days we add up the commission you saved and your direct order revenue. If it is less than 3X our service fee, we refund 100% of what you paid.
