          FAQs

          Questions Owners Ask Us First

          How does the loyalty program work?

          Customers earn points or stamps on every direct order and redeem them for rewards you choose, like a free dessert. It runs on your own ordering website and WhatsApp.

          Do I have to stop selling on Zomato and Swiggy?

          No. Keep them for new customers. We move your regulars to your own program so their next orders come without commission.

          How do app customers find out about it?

          Inserts in every app parcel, QR codes on tables and bills, and WhatsApp messages to the customers who join.

          Can I choose the rewards?

          Yes. You decide the points, tiers and rewards, and we set them up so they fit your margins.

          How does the 3X guarantee work?

          Over 90 days we add up the commission you saved and the revenue from your direct orders. If it is less than 3X our service fee, we refund 100% of what you paid.

          Do I need any technical knowledge?

          None. We set up the program, the rewards and the campaigns. You focus on your kitchen.
