          FAQs

          Questions Owners Ask Us First

          Is it like the Zomato or Swiggy ordering experience?

          Yes. Customers browse your menu, add items and pay online in a few taps, on your own branded website.

          Do I have to stop selling on Zomato and Swiggy?

          No. Keep them for new customers and move your regulars to direct ordering.

          Does RestroGrow take a commission on my orders?

          No. There is no per order platform commission to RestroGrow. Every direct order is yours. A small payment gateway fee applies on online payments.

          How will customers find my website?

          We put it where they already see you: inserts in app parcels, QR codes on tables, WhatsApp, your Google profile and social media.

          Who delivers direct orders?

          We set up delivery to fit your outlet, with your own riders or a delivery partner. We walk you through the options in the demo.

          How does the 3X guarantee work?

          Over 90 days we add up the commission you saved and your direct order revenue. If it is less than 3X our service fee, we refund 100% of what you paid.
