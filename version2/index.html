FAQs

Questions Owners Ask Us First

Do I have to stop selling on Zomato and Swiggy?+

No. Keep them for new customers. RestroGrow moves your regulars to direct ordering, so their next orders come without commission.

How does the 3X guarantee work?+

Over 90 days, we add up the commission you saved and the revenue from your direct orders. If it's less than 3X our service fee, we refund 100% of what you paid us.

Does RestroGrow take a commission on my orders?+

No. There is no per order platform commission to RestroGrow. Every direct order is yours.

How will my customers find my ordering website?+

We put it everywhere they already see you: inserts in every app parcel, QR codes on tables, WhatsApp offers, your Google profile and social media.

Who delivers direct orders?+

We set up delivery to fit your outlet, with your own riders or a delivery partner. We'll walk you through the options in the demo.

Do I need any technical knowledge?+

None. We set up the system, your menu and the campaigns. You focus on your kitchen.
